# Governance

cloud-itonami-isic-5911 follows open-source governance with transparent
decision-making documented in ADRs.

Scope: Back-office production-record logging, shoot-day/location/crew
scheduling proposals, on-set safety-concern flagging (always
human-escalated), and post-production footage/dailies handoff
coordination.

Out-of-scope (permanent, structural, not a rollout milestone): finalizing
an on-set safety-clearance decision (stunt-clearance sign-off,
hazard-clearance sign-off), overriding a minor-performer work-hour
limit, direct actuation of rigging/stunt/pyrotechnic equipment, and
talent-compensation/insurance/legal/union adjudication.

See README.md for complete CRITICAL scope exclusions.
